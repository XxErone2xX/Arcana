## Arcana V0.9.8 Update

- Fixed: after restarting the app, the Settings window showed default values (the real settings were saved correctly)
  - Cause: while the app was still loading (brain, chat model, notes) the window asked for the settings too early, failed, and kept the defaults
  - The window now waits until the app is ready and then shows the saved settings, even if Settings is opened right away
