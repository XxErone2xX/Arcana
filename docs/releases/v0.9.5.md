## Arcana V0.9.5 Update

- Added: converter for AI Hub's "call center QA set" dataset (`training\import_aihub_callcenter.py`)
  - Turns customer → agent exchanges into conversation pairs in the `data` folder (stage-2 conversation training)
  - Converts agent replies to Arcana's formal style, e.g. "확인해 보세요" → "확인해 보십시오", "했나요?" → "했습니까?"
  - Cleans up translation artifacts and trims replies to what the model can see at once (about 150 characters)
